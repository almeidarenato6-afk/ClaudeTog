No binary icon assets are included in this scaffold.

Expected assets (to be supplied by design, then wired via a drawables.xml resource
definition in this directory):
  - launcher_icon.png  — 40x40 (and @2x/@3x variants per target device screen density)
  - action_menu_icon.png — used in GameModeView's category menu items, ~30x30

Once real PNGs are added, declare them in resources/drawables/drawables.xml, e.g.:

  <resources>
      <drawables>
          <drawable id="LauncherIcon" filename="launcher_icon.png" />
      </drawables>
  </resources>

and reference as @Drawables.LauncherIcon (already used in ../../manifest.xml).
